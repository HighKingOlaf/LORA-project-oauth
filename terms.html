Terms of service

LORA.Project is a personal-use encrypted backup utility, not a public software-as-a-service offering.

1. Intended use

The utility is intended for the owner's private backup, integrity verification, and restoration of project-memory archives using their own authorized Google Drive account. This website is informational and does not itself store, serve, or restore backup content.

2. Account access and authorization

Google account access requires the owner's consent through Google's OAuth authorization flow. The operator is responsible for protecting local credentials, encryption keys, Google account access, and the device that runs the utility. Authorization can be revoked through Google account permissions.

3. Backups and responsibility

Backups and recovery procedures should be periodically verified. No backup process can guarantee uninterrupted availability or recovery from every possible failure. The operator should maintain independent backup copies and protect encryption keys.

4. Third-party services

Google Drive supplies cloud storage and Google OAuth authorization. GitHub Pages hosts these informational pages. Their respective terms and privacy policies also apply.

5. Availability and changes

The utility and informational site are provided for private use on an “as available” basis. Features, documentation, and internal operating procedures may change. The site does not offer public registration, subscriptions, or paid access.

6. Contact

For non-sensitive questions, use the public project issue tracker. Do not publish private account details, backup files, or credentials there.
